Import your Audible library

Bring in every book in your Audible library, with its length and whether you've finished it, in a minute or two. You'll need a computer for this part.

Open this page on a computer

Bookmarks like this one run in a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari), not in the Audible app or a phone's browser. On your phone you can use the Quick start instead: in Earmark, tap + and then Quick start from Audible.

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar Earmark: import Audible

    Can't see your bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl Shift B.

    To add it by hand: copy it, make a new bookmark (any page will do), and paste what you copied as its address (URL). Name it Earmark.

  2. Open your Audible library

    Go to audible.com, sign in the way you usually do, and open Library.

    Open audible.com/library
  3. Click the Earmark bookmark

    It reads your library a page at a time (you'll see its progress in the corner) and opens Earmark in a new tab.

  4. Check the list in Earmark, then tap Add

    Nothing is saved until you do. Books already in Earmark are left as they are, so nothing counts twice.

Your password stays with Audible

The bookmark works on the Audible page you're already signed in to. It reads only your library list and hands it to Earmark in your own browser. It never sees, asks for or sends your Amazon or Audible password, and Earmark will never ask for it.

What comes in

  • Finished books, with their full length. Audible's library doesn't say when you finished them, so they come in without a date: they count toward your lifetime total and book medals, never toward streaks.
  • Books you're partway through go to Listening.
  • Books you haven't started go to Want to listen. You can untick any of these before adding.

If you typed in your total from the Audible app's Stats, Earmark offers to swap it for these books, so the same hours aren't counted twice. For the dates you finished each book and every day you listened, use Complete history in Earmark (Settings › Import books), from Amazon's data request.
