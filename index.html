Welcome to Earmark

Track every hour you listen, earn medals along the way, and fill a shelf with what you've finished.

Step 1 of 3

Hours you've already listened

If you have hours from Audible, Libby, or anywhere else, add them so your rank starts where you are. No date is attached and they never count toward streaks.

Step 2 of 3

Books you've finished

Add a few favourites to start your shelf. Hours are optional; when you add them they count toward your total.

Step 3 of 3

You're set.

Tap the gold + whenever you finish a session to log today's listening. Your streak starts with day one, and your first 25 hours earn Bronze.
