    Privacy

    Step 1 of 3

    Hours you've already listened

    If you have hours from Audible, Libby, or anywhere else, add them so your rank starts where you are. No date is attached and they never count toward streaks.

    Step 2 of 3

    Books you've finished

    Search for a few favourites to start your shelf. Their hours count toward your total.

    Step 3 of 3

    You're set.

    Tap the + whenever you finish a session to log today's listening. Your streak starts with day one, and your first 25 hours earn Bronze.
