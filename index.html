    Hours you've already listened

    If you have hours from Audible, Libby, or anywhere else, add them so your rank starts where you are. No date is attached and they never count toward streaks.

    Quick start from Audible
    1. Open the Audible app on your phone.
    2. Tap Profile, then Stats.
    3. Your listening time is at the top. If you can pick a period, choose All time.

    Type it in as it's shown. If you import your Audible history later, Earmark offers to swap this total for it, so nothing counts twice.

    Import books
    From Audible
    Your Audible libraryEvery book, its length and whether you finished it, with a bookmark on a computer.

    Amazon's data request: on Amazon, go to Account › Request Your Data, choose Audible, and confirm the email. When it arrives, choose the file here (it may be called File_000). Saved your library from the bookmark as a file instead? .

    From other apps

    Goodreads: on the website, My Books › Import and export › Export Library. StoryGraph: Manage Account › Manage Your Data › Export StoryGraph Library. Libby: Shelf › Timeline › Actions › Export Timeline, as a spreadsheet or data. Libby doesn't record finishing, so its books use the day you returned them.

    Quick start from Audible

    1. Open the Audible app on your phone.
    2. Tap Profile, then Stats.
    3. Your listening time is at the top. If you can pick a period, choose All time.

    Added to your lifetime total and rank, never to streaks. If you import your Audible history later, Earmark offers to swap this total for it, so nothing counts twice.

    Hours from the past

    No date attached. Adds to your lifetime total and rank, never to streaks.

    Edit book

    Changing the length updates your lifetime total, rank and medals. Books without a date count toward your total but never toward streaks.

    Restore from backup

    Merge adds books and listening from the backup that aren't here yet. Replace swaps everything for the backup.
